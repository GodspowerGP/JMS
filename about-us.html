Who We Are

Jewel Model School, established in 1996 in Kubwa, Abuja, is a qualitative educational institution comprising Nursery, Primary, and Secondary schools, with branches across Nigeria. Focused on blending quality education with strict discipline, the school caters to diverse income levels and maintains zero tolerance for examination malpractice, ensuring graduates can defend their certificates.

See Our Branches

Brief Introduction

Jewel Model School was founded on 26 April 1996 as a Nursery and Primary School in Kukwaba, Kubwa Village. The school was incorporated in November 1998 with RC No. 345874, while the Secondary School arm was established in September 1998. The Primary School received approval from the FCT Administration in 2001, and the Secondary School was accredited in 2003 to conduct the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE) and the National Examination Council (NECO) Senior Secondary Certificate Examination.

At Jewel Model School, we combine the Nigerian educational curriculum with British and American best educational practices to raise students who are academically sound, morally upright, and globally competitive. We also promote godliness, moral discipline, and peaceful coexistence through the teaching of Christian and Islamic Religious Studies, fostering mutual respect among all people.
