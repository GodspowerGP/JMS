Welcome to Jewel Model School

Our English-medium school is dedicated to providing high-quality, internationally focused education that fosters intellectual growth, personal development and global citizenship. Guided by a clear mission to equip students with the knowledge, skills and values needed for success in a dynamic world, we emphasise academic excellence, character building and lifelong learning. We are driven by core values of integrity, respect, innovation and responsibility — creating a supportive and inclusive environment where every student can thrive.
