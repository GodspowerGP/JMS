The Golden Sunset

Amina Bello (SS2) Published: June 2026
The skies are painted gold and red, As weary birds prepare for bed. A quiet whisper in the breeze, Rustles through the sleeping trees. The sun begins its slow descent, A day of light and labor spent. It leaves behind a glowing trace, Of warmth upon the earth's soft face. And as the shadows start to grow, The world takes on a gentle glow. A promise made of stars and night, Before the return of morning light.

Echoes of the Classroom

Chidi Okafor (JS3) Published: May 2026
The bell rings out, a sudden sound, And active footsteps run around. The heavy books, the wooden desk, The chalk and charts that we know best. We solve the math, we write the page, As learning guides us through this age. But more than lessons that we find, Are bonds of friendship left behind. The shared laugh under noon-day sun, The games we played when class was done. These echoes ring, and they will stay, Long after we have walked away.
