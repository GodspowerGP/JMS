The Admission Process

We've made joining the Jewel family simple and transparent.

1

Obtain an Application Form

Application forms can be purchased at the administrative office of any of our campuses (Kubwa, Kagini, or Primary) during working hours.

2

Entrance Examination

Prospective students will be scheduled for a comprehensive entrance examination to assess their current academic standing in Mathematics, English, and General Aptitude.

3

Parent & Student Interview

Successful candidates and their parents will be invited for a brief, informal interview with the school administration to align on values and expectations.

4

Offer & Registration

An official offer of admission is extended. Upon payment of the necessary fees and submission of required documents, the student is officially registered into JMS.
