Welcome to JMS

Raising the
Next Generation

Jewel Model School was founded on 26 April 1996 in Kukwaba, Kubwa Village, Abuja. What began as a humble Nursery and Primary School has grown into one of Abuja's most respected educational institutions.

We combine the Nigerian educational curriculum with British and American best practices to ensure our students are prepared for the global stage — promoting godliness, moral discipline, and peaceful coexistence.
